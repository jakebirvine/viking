How it works

The same seven steps, every job

Scroll and the background builds a yard in the same order we do. No surprises, no "while we're here" invoices at the end.

  1. 01

    Site walk & free quote

    We come out, walk the block, take levels and listen to what you actually want. You get a written fixed price with the inclusions spelled out — usually inside a week.

  2. 02

    Excavation & prep

    Old concrete, dead lawn and rubbish out. Bobcat in, levels cut, base compacted. Every job lives or dies on what happens in this step.

  3. 03

    Formwork & pour

    Boxing set to fall, mesh and bar chairs in, then the pour. Screeded, floated and finished the way you picked — broom, honed or exposed aggregate.

  4. 04

    Walls, edges & drainage

    Retaining walls footed and backfilled, ag-drain laid, garden edging run dead straight. This is where a yard stops looking like a DIY job.

  5. 05

    Turf laid

    Topsoil spread and screeded, turf rolled tight with staggered joints, then rolled and watered in. Walk on it that evening, mow it in a fortnight.

  6. 06

    Planting & mulch

    Beds dug, plants set out and stepped back on before anything goes in the ground. Mulched 75mm deep so it holds moisture through summer.

  7. 07

    Clean-up & handover

    Every offcut, bag and barrow load goes on our trailer. We walk you through the irrigation timer, the watering-in schedule and how to keep it looking like this.

Questions

The ones we get asked every week

Is the quote actually free?

Yes. We come out, measure up and give you a written fixed price. No call-out fee, no obligation, and we don't do the "sign today or the price goes up" routine.

How long does a typical backyard take?

A standard new-estate backyard — slab, wall, turf and beds — is usually five to eight working days on site. Concrete needs curing time in the middle of that, so allow a fortnight end to end. Weather out here can add a day or two.

When can I walk on new concrete and new turf?

Turf, the same evening — light foot traffic only for the first fortnight while it roots in. Concrete, 24–48 hours for walking and seven days before you park a car on it. Full strength is 28 days, and we'll leave you a curing rundown.

Do you handle permits and crossovers?

We work with both Wyndham City and Geelong council requirements regularly, including vehicle crossovers. We'll tell you upfront what's needed for your job and what it costs — it's on the quote, not a surprise later.

What about the clay out here?

Most of Wyndham is reactive clay, which is exactly why base prep and drainage matter more here than they do closer to town. We compact properly, use ag-drain behind walls and set falls away from the house. It's not glamorous work but it's the reason things stay put.

Do you take the rubbish away?

Always. Spoil, old concrete, offcuts, empty bags — it leaves on our trailer. You shouldn't have to book a skip to finish someone else's job.

Do you do small jobs?

Yep. A path, a few square metres of turf, one wall, a garden clean-up — happy to price it. Small jobs are how most of our big ones started.

How do I pay?

Deposit to lock in the date, progress payment at a milestone on larger builds, balance on completion once you've walked it with us and you're happy. Bank transfer or card.
